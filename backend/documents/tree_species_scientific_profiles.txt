ENCYCLOPEDIA OF TROPICAL URBAN TREES: BIODIVERSITY, MEDICINAL PROPERTIES, AND HABITAT SUPPORT

SECTION 1: AZADIRACHTA INDICA (NEEM)
1.1 Ecological Profile & Habitat Support
Neem (Azadirachta indica) is a crucial component of tropical urban and rural ecosystems. Its canopy structure provides essential thermal shelter, nesting opportunities, and foraging substrates for regional avifauna and arboreal mammals. The flowers and fruits provide high-value nectar, pollen, and nourishment for native honeybees, solitary wasps, butterflies, and frugivorous wildlife, sustaining vibrant urban biodiversity corridors.
1.2 Medicinal & Pharmacological Impact
In traditional and modern pharmacological sciences, Neem provides notable therapeutic bio-compounds: Limonoids (Azadirachtin, Nimbin, Salannin, Gedunin, Nimbolide), quercetin, and catechins.. Broad-spectrum antibacterial, antifungal, antiviral, anti-inflammatory, and insecticidal agent. Used for eczema, acne, gingivitis, wound healing, and blood purification. Documented in traditional pharmacopoeias and validated by clinical PubMed trials: Over 1,200 PubMed studies validating dental plaque reduction, dermatological healing, and organic pest control.
