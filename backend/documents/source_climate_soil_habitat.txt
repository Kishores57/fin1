=== SPECIES: AZADIRACHTA INDICA (NEEM) ===
1. Habitat Information
- Natural Habitat: Dry deciduous forests, thorny scrublands, and open savannah woodlands.
- Forest Type: Tropical dry deciduous forest and thorn scrub.
- Ecosystem: Adapted to arid and semi-arid plains, riverbanks, and disturbed urban ecosystems.
- Preferred Elevation: Sea level up to 700 meters, occasionally up to 1,500 meters.
- Moisture Requirements: Drought-resistant; prefers moderate to low moisture. Intolerant of waterlogging or stagnant flooding.

2. Climate Preference
- Climate Zone: Tropical and subtropical dry zones (Köppen: Aw, BSh, Cwa).
- Average Temperature: Annual mean temperature between 21°C and 32°C. Tolerates extreme heat up to 48°C and down to 4°C.
- Rainfall Range: Annual rainfall of 450mm to 1,200mm is optimal; survives in low zones down to 150mm with deep taproot.
- Humidity: Low to moderate relative humidity (30% to 65%).
- Tolerances: High drought and heat tolerance; extremely low tolerance to frost and persistent waterlogging.

3. Soil Requirements
- Soil Type: Sandy, stony, clayey, saline, and nutrient-poor dry soils.
- Soil Texture: Prefers deep sandy loams for optimal root development, tolerates rocky substrates.
- Soil pH: pH ranges from 6.0 to 8.5 (slightly acidic to highly alkaline).
- Soil Organic Carbon: Survives in low organic carbon soils (<0.5%), growth accelerates in organically rich loams.
- Soil Drainage: Well-drained, sandy-aerated soils required; root rot occurs in stagnant heavy clays.

=== SPECIES: SARACA ASOCA (ASHOKA) ===
1. Habitat Information
- Natural Habitat: Shady understory of tropical rain forests, riverine buffer zones, and coastal green belts.
- Forest Type: Tropical wet evergreen and semi-evergreen rain forests.
- Ecosystem: Riparian stream corridors, humid mountain valleys, and sacred grove sanctuaries.
- Preferred Elevation: Elevations from 50 meters up to 750 meters.
- Moisture Requirements: Requires high soil moisture and continuous atmospheric humidity; sensitive to dry seasonal winds.

2. Climate Preference
- Climate Zone: Humid tropical zone (Köppen: Af, Am, monsoon belts).
- Average Temperature: Annual mean temperature of 24°C to 32°C. Cold-sensitive; temperatures below 10°C cause leaf damage.
- Rainfall Range: Optimal annual rainfall of 2,000mm to 4,500mm with short or absent dry season.
- Humidity: High relative humidity (70% to 90% year-round).
- Tolerances: Low drought tolerance; low frost tolerance; high shade tolerance; moderate seasonal riparian flood tolerance.

3. Soil Requirements
- Soil Type: Deep alluvial, loamy, and humic clay-loam forest soils.
- Soil Texture: Silty clay loams and alluvial sandy clay loams rich in decomposing leaf litter.
- Soil pH: Slightly acidic to neutral pH (5.5 to 7.0).
- Soil Organic Carbon: High organic carbon content (>1.5% in topsoil) essential for vigorous growth.
- Soil Drainage: Moisture-retentive yet well-aerated; intolerant of stagnant, oxygen-depleted standing water.
